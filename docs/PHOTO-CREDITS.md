# Photo credits

The photos of places in Iloilo City and Iloilo Province come from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org), where photographers share
them under Creative Commons licences. Those licences let us use the photos
**as long as we credit the photographer and link the licence**.

The credits are shown on the site at `/credits`, linked from the footer
("Photo credits"). The list lives in `src/lib/photo-credits.ts`: if you add a
photo from Commons, add an entry there. Your own photos need no credit.

## Places still showing a stock photo

No free photo of these places exists on Commons yet, so they still show a
generic stock photo from Unsplash: Agony Hill, Balog Falls, Casanalay Falls,
Garita Farm, Mt. Nangtud, Mt. Opao, Sibunag Agri-Tourism Park, Tiniaman
Falls, Mama's Kitchen, Hotel del Rio and Richmonde Hotel Iloilo.
