# 102 — The notifications bell and its list

**What to build:** When someone replies to a traveler's review or answers their reply, the database creates a notification for them (never for their own reply). Logged in, a bell sits beside the profile icon (in the phone menu, a "Notifications" line), with a small red dot and number when something is unread. Clicking it opens a glass list, newest first, up to 20: "Maria replied to your review of Bucari Little Baguio" or "Juan answered your reply on Hotel del Rio", with how long ago. Empty: "No notifications yet." Each traveler can only ever read their own notifications.

**Blocked by:** None

**Fix (2026-11-07, team report "scrolling the notifications also scrolls the home page" on a laptop):** root cause: "scroll chaining". The dropdown's list scrolls inside its own box; at its top or bottom, when it is too short to scroll, or over the heading, the browser passed the scroll on to the page behind it. Now the list has overscroll-contain, and while the dropdown is open a wheel listener stops any scroll that would leave it: inside the list it lets the list scroll, and only blocks at its top or bottom; over the heading or "Mark all as read" it always blocks. The phone menu already scrolls on its own (fix above).
