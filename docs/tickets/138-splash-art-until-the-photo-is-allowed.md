# 138 — Splash art until the photo is allowed

**What to build:** Travelers only see a place's own photo once the team has saved its credits with an authorization status other than "Not checked yet" (Allowed by its licence, Permission from the owner, or Our team's own photo). Until then — including places with no credits yet — the home page cards, the Featured and Trending cards and the place page show the Lakat Iloilo splash art instead: the team's wide 1920 × 1080 art. The photo stays uploaded and the dashboard still shows it, so saving allowed credits later shows it at once, with no new upload.
