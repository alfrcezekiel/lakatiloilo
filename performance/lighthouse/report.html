Home TBT on a phone: a small, real miss

Today's five runs on the charger gave home TBT of 325, 237, 284, 186 and 246 ms, so 4 of 5 are over the limit by 40–125 ms. This is not noise. The busy time is one script, the React and Next.js framework chunk (04by0qkjl0sry.js): 615 ms of script work while React brings the page to life, including one 297 ms long task. The fix is to give home less to bring to life (fewer or lighter parts that run in the browser). That is a change across the page, so it is its own ticket.

Next step

Both misses have the same root cause: the JavaScript the phone has to download and run. Ticket 158, "lighter pages on a phone" (docs/tickets/158-lighter-pages-on-a-phone.md), covers both. It should start by measuring what is in each script chunk, then move the parts that are not needed straight away to load later. The budgets stay at Google's limits.
